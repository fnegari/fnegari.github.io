01GRADUATE RESEARCH · ONGOING

Finding patterns in
complex operations

Using data mining to explore Functional Resonance Analysis Method (FRAM) outputs, with Arctic ship-route planning as the case study. The work connects system-level questions with Python-based engineering data analysis.

FRAMPythonpandasArctic routing
Inside the research

The questionWhat patterns can be found in FRAM outputs, and how can those patterns help describe the behaviour of a complex operational system?

My contributionI analyse the outputs and am developing FRAM software alongside the research. In June 2026, I presented “Identifying Hidden Decision Patterns in Arctic Ship Route Planning” at the 18th FRAMily Meeting & Workshop, Technical University of Munich.

Current workThe data analysis and FRAM software development are part of my ongoing graduate research.
